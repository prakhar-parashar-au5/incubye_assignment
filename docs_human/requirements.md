
## Primary User : 

HRs of the company

## Goal : 

HRs should not have to rely on excel sheets for managing employees compensations records. To provide clean UI, working on desktop and mobile, with which they can search, filter out employees and their salary records. Make ammends, check history of who changed what. They should also be able to track increaments, bonuses. The portal should also help them decide how much salary compensation to give during review cycles. 


## Workflow : 

An HR admin signs in, sees basic ledger with all employees records with their emp id, name, salary, hiring date, etc in paginated format. The HR searches in a search box with emp id, name, email , etc. Filters and sorting options are present, to filter on basis of departments, countries. They should get options against every employee row to make updates,which takes them to a form. The admin HR can see an option named 'history' where a record is present for every change made. The history should be in easy readable format eg, "Alekh panded updated salary of piyush gupta from 20,00,000 to 22,00,000.
