Traced at master 9236ac4 with AI Task 05 merged (feat/architecture-flows). Generated from flows/F3-salary-request-approval.json.

  • Audit writes: none in this flow. access_audit_events is written only by HR access management (hr-users.ts). The salary audit trail is the rows themselves: the request (requester, versions, key) and the new compensation_records version (changed_by_hr_user_id, source_request_id, previous_compensation_record_id).
  • Idempotency keys: request_idempotency_key on the request insert (step 19 replay check, step 21 insert); decision_idempotency_key on approval (step 32 replay check, step 36 update) and operation_idempotency_key on the new compensation record (step 35).
  • Every request also passes requireAuth (shown once at step 2), which reloads the principal from MySQL; steps 16, 24, and 30 go through it the same way.
  • Admin HR cannot submit a request (403 at the route; Admin applies directly), and Scoped HR cannot approve (403 at the approve route).
